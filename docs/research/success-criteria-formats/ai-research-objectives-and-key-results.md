## Strengths and Limitations; Analysis
- Strength: Connects measurable outcomes to the broader purpose.
- Limitation: High-level or aspirational goals may be unsuitable as minimum product acceptance conditions; outcomes can depend on factors outside the app.

## Project Relevance; Analysis
Useful for checking whether proposed criteria represent easier organization rather than only more features.

## Illustrative Application
> Objective: reduce organization effort. Key result: a predefined improvement in measured effort during a controlled user task.

This example is authored for comparison. It is not taken from the source and does not establish the project's final criteria or thresholds.

## Useful Lesson
Borrow the link between purpose and measurable outcomes without treating aspirational targets as mandatory acceptance gates.
