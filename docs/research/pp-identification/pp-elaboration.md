# Personal Project Elaboration

## a) Definition

An independent, student-led inquiry in which you pursue a personal interest, achieve a learning goal, create a product, and reflect on the process.

## b) Components; Verbose

1. Process:

> planning, research, decisions, progress, challenges, and evidence

2. Product or outcome:

> evidence of tangible or intangible results

3. Report:

> an account of the project and its impact, to a structure that follows the assessment criteria; describes both the process of creating the project and an evaluation of the impact of the process on the student or their learning

## c) Components; Full

1. Learning goal: Crit A
2. Product: Crit C
3. Success criteria: Crit A & C
4. Action plan: Crit A
5. Process journal: Crit A & B & C
6. Bibliography: Required(no criterion)
7. Final Report: Root

## d) Chronological Workflow

1. Initial ideas
2. Define project: learning goal, product goal, and personal interest
3. Product success criteria
4. Action plan
5. Research and creation of product
6. Draft submission
7. Showcase
8. Final submission

## e) Assessment Expectations

### Submission

**ONLY THE FINAL REPORT**

### Criterion

1. Criterion A: Planning
   - States a learning goal and explains the connection between personal interests and that goal.
   - States the intended product and presents multiple appropriate, detailed success criteria.
   - Presents a detailed plan covering the product and all associated success criteria.
2. Criterion B: Applying Skills
   - Explains how ATL skills helped achieve the learning goal, supported by detailed examples or evidence.
   - Explains how ATL skills helped achieve the product, supported by detailed examples or evidence.
3. Criterion C: Reflecting
   - Explains the project’s impact on themselves or their learning.
   - Evaluates the product against the success criteria, fully supported by specific evidence or detailed examples.

## f) Report Specifications

### Format

- Maximum 15 pages, including visual evidence
- Minimum 11-point font
- Minimum 2 cm margins
- No required page size or word count
- Accepted written formats: DOC, DOCX, PDF, or RTF
- Organized under Criteria A, B, and C
- Combines concise prose with tables, images, screenshots, and annotations
- Bibliography submitted separately

### Sections

> Follows criterion A & B & C

- Planning
- Applying Skills
- Reflecting
