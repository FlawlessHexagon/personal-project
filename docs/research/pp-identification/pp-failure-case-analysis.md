# Personal Project Failure Case Analysis

## a) Scope and Evidence Limits

- Dataset: 2 records in [failure-cases.json](./pp-failure-case/failure-cases.json)
- Case 1: lower achievement, with a public score of 10/24
- Case 2: partial success-criteria failure, with no public score
- The sample is too small for broad conclusions.

## b) Source-Supported Findings

1. Weak time management caused rushed work and incomplete documentation.
2. Late or irregular process-journal entries reduced available evidence.
3. Insufficient early research produced unclear direction or unrealistic constraints.
4. Technical and financial risks were not tested early enough.
5. Product completion did not compensate for unmet success criteria.

## c) Practical Lessons

1. Define measurable and realistic success criteria.
2. Research scope, costs, tools, and risks before committing.
3. Test critical equipment and assumptions early.
4. Set firm milestones for the product and report.
5. Maintain the process journal every week.
6. Preserve contingency time and budget.

## d) Inference

The two cases suggest that planning and documentation failures can weaken an otherwise viable product. This is a limited observation, not a general pattern established by the dataset.
