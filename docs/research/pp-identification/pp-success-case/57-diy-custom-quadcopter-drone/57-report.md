# [57] Custom Remote-Controlled Quadcopter Drone

## Classification
- Topic: Robotics & aerospace engineering / embedded hardware integration
- Evidence status: Official San Juan USD MYP Personal Project Showcase 2020/2021 STEM entry; showcase-only

## Project
- Goal: Research aerodynamics, electronics, and flight avionics to engineer and test a fully functional custom quadcopter drone.
- Product: An assembled, calibrated, and flight-tested quadcopter integrating brushless motors, electronic speed controllers (ESCs), and radio telemetry.

## Why it succeeded
- Overcame electrical wiring challenges, power distribution limits, and gyro calibration to achieve stable controlled flight.
- Validated operational delivery via showcase exhibition in the district STEM portfolio (no public IB score is available).

## Useful lesson
Systematic motor thrust-to-weight ratio calculations prior to component assembly prevent motor overheating and insufficient vertical lift.
