# [52] BioPrinter Tissue Scaffold Platform

## Classification
- Topic: Biomedical engineering & digital fabrication / additive manufacturing
- Evidence status: Official Overseas Family School MYP Personal Project Exhibition 2021 entry; showcase-only

## Project
- Goal: Design and assemble an experimental 3D bioprinter tailored for depositing biocompatible hydrogels and cellular scaffolding.
- Product: A functional benchtop bioprinter adapting multi-axis CNC motion control to precision syringe extrusion for biomaterials.

## Why it succeeded
- Re-engineered fluid displacement and extruder mechanics to handle viscoelastic fluid properties without clogging.
- Verified precision deposition across test beds, earning showcase exhibition honors (no public IB score is available).

## Useful lesson
Adapting standard FDM motion axes to fluid biomaterials requires fine calibration of syringe back-pressure and volumetric flow rates.
