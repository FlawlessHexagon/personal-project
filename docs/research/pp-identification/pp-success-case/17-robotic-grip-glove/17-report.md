# [17] Biomechanical Robotic Grip Glove

## Classification
- Topic: Robotics / biomedical augmentation & hardware
- Evidence status: Official Northville High School MYP showcase spotlight; showcase-only

## Project
- Goal: Explore human body biomechanical augmentation by engineering a wearable robotic glove that enhances user hand grip strength.
- Product: A compact wearable robotic glove with mechanical actuators designed to measure and augment human gripping force.

## Why it succeeded
Featured showcase selection on the Northville High School MYP project portal is the only success signal; selection and publication is the only success signal and no score is public.

## Useful lesson
Establishing measurable before-and-after physical metrics (grip force with vs without glove) creates clear Criterion C evaluation evidence.
