# [51] The ARM Bionic Prosthetic

## Classification
- Topic: Robotics & biomechatronics / prosthetic engineering
- Evidence status: Official Overseas Family School MYP Personal Project Exhibition 2021 entry; showcase-only

## Project
- Goal: Design and fabricate an articulated, motorized prosthetic arm capable of functional grasping at an affordable cost.
- Product: The ARM, a multi-segment prosthetic limb incorporating servo actuation, tendon cable routing, and mechanical grip linkages.

## Why it succeeded
- Overcame structural tolerance issues through rapid iterative 3D printing and mechanical tension tuning.
- Completed functional grasping demonstrations validated project criteria and secured inclusion in the MYP exhibition (no public IB score is available).

## Useful lesson
Physical cable-driven tension mechanisms demand precise mechanical anchoring to translate motor torque into reliable end-effector grip force.
