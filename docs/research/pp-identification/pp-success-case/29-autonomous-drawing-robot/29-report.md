# [29] Autonomous Precision Drawing Robot

## Classification
- Topic: Robotics & embedded systems / mechatronics
- Evidence status: Official San Juan USD MYP Personal Project Showcase 2020/2021 STEM entry; showcase-only

## Project
- Goal: Design, 3D print, and program a microcontroller-driven robotic plotter capable of translating digital vector paths into physical drawings.
- Product: A completed autonomous drawing robot integrating stepper motor controls, servo pen lifters, and custom microcontroller firmware.

## Why it succeeded
District MYP STEM showcase publication is the only success signal and no score is public.

## Useful lesson
Mechatronic projects require rigorous calibration between digital stepper motor coordinates and physical friction constraints.
