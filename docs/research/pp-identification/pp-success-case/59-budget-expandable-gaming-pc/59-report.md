# [59] Expandable Modular Gaming PC Architecture

## Classification
- Topic: Computer engineering & modular hardware design / budget optimization
- Evidence status: Official San Juan USD MYP Personal Project Showcase 2020/2021 Fabrication entry; showcase-only

## Project
- Goal: Design and assemble a budget-constrained gaming computer architected specifically for staged, long-term modular component upgrades.
- Product: A balance-optimized desktop PC built on an expandable motherboard platform with high-capacity power headroom for future GPU scaling.

## Why it succeeded
- Carefully balanced price-to-performance metrics while avoiding proprietary form factor lock-in.
- Completed system assembly, BIOS configuration, and stress testing, earning showcase publication (no public IB score is available).

## Useful lesson
Investing in a reliable, over-spec power supply unit and an open standard motherboard form factor preserves upgradability across hardware generations.
