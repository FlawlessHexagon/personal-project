# [50] DAD: Dementia Assisting Device

## Classification
- Topic: Assistive technology & embedded systems / healthcare hardware
- Evidence status: Official Overseas Family School MYP Personal Project Exhibition 2021 entry; showcase-only

## Project
- Goal: Engineer a dedicated assistive hardware device to help individuals with dementia retain daily routines and memory cues.
- Product: DAD (Dementia Assisting Device), a specialized interactive reminder unit featuring scheduled audio-visual prompts and orientation aids.

## Why it succeeded
- Grounded in direct user-need analysis, the design prioritized high-contrast clarity and predictable interaction patterns.
- Demonstrated end-to-end execution from design specifications to physical prototype, earning exhibition showcase selection (no public IB score is available).

## Useful lesson
Assistive technology products must rigidly constrain feature complexity to suit the cognitive bandwidth of their target users.
