# [15] Northville Charity Chess Tournament

## Classification
- Topic: School and club organizing / philanthropic event management
- Evidence status: Official Northville High School MYP showcase spotlight; showcase-only

## Project
- Goal: Organize and host a scholastic chess tournament to build regional community engagement and raise charitable funds for women in chess.
- Product: The inaugural Northville Charity Chess Tournament, convening approximately thirty scholastic players at the district library and raising funds for the USCF Youth Scholastic Women in Chess Initiative.

## Why it succeeded
Featured showcase publication on the school MYP portal documenting successful event execution is the only success signal; selection and publication is the only success signal and no score is public.

## Useful lesson
Club and community events succeed when logistics, external venue partnerships, and charitable fundraising are planned with structured milestones.
