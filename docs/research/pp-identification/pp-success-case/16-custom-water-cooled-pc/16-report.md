# [16] Custom Water-Cooled Extreme PC

## Classification
- Topic: Computer hardware engineering / thermal systems design
- Evidence status: Official Northville High School MYP showcase spotlight; showcase-only

## Project
- Goal: Master advanced computer electronics, high-stress hardware assembly, and thermodynamic cooling loops by building an extreme performance PC.
- Product: A custom-engineered, wall-mounted, water-cooled personal computer with a hand-designed liquid cooling loop for high-stress rendering.

## Why it succeeded
Showcase spotlight feature on the official Northville High School MYP portal is the only success signal; selection and publication is the only success signal and no score is public.

## Useful lesson
Complex physical engineering tasks benefit from deep research into component compatibility, custom fabrication, and systematic leak testing.
