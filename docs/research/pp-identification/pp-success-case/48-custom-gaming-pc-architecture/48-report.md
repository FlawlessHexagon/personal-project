# [48] High-Performance PC Hardware Architecture

## Classification
- Topic: Computer engineering & hardware architecture / systems integration
- Evidence status: Official San Juan USD MYP Personal Project Showcase 2020/2021 STEM entry; showcase-only

## Project
- Goal: Master computer hardware architecture, component compatibility, power distribution, and thermal management under budget constraints.
- Product: A fully assembled, configured, and benchmarked high-performance desktop workstation optimized for heavy computing workloads.

## Why it succeeded
- The student executed a systematic investigation into bus speeds, power supply load curves, and thermal throttling.
- The assembled rig successfully completed operating system deployment and hardware stress tests.
- High school showcase exhibition validates project completion (no public IB score is available).

## Useful lesson
Detailed compatibility and wattage budgeting prior to part procurement prevents costly electrical and physical integration failures.
