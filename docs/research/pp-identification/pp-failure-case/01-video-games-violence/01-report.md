# [01] Video games and violence awareness campaign

## Classification
Lower-achievement case (official score 10/24; not a total failure).

## Project
An awareness campaign and video investigating whether violent video games cause aggression, intended to include posters, an in-school trial, and a video product.

## What failed
The student failed product success criteria: the video lacked depth to shift opinions and missed aesthetic quality goals. Process failures included losing interview footage due to technical errors, procrastinating, and rushing report writing into the final week.

## Likely causes (source fact vs inference)
- Source facts: The student acknowledged poor time management, unrecorded interviews requiring re-recording, struggling to form a solid research question, and cramming the report during final eAssessments.
- Inference: Ill-defined success criteria and lack of interim milestones caused evaluation and reflection scores to suffer, resulting in 10/24.

## Prevention lesson
Establish measurable criteria beyond length, test recording equipment early, and enforce firm phase deadlines to avoid deferring report writing to the last week.
