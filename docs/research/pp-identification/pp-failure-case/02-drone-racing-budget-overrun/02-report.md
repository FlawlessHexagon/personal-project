# [02] First-person view racing drone build

## Classification
Partial success-criteria failure (no public score reported).

## Project
An engineering capstone to construct an FPV racing quadcopter, learn high-speed piloting, and enter a competitive league event.

## What failed
The project suffered a 6x budget overrun, escalating from an initial $250 criterion to roughly $1,500 in total expenditures to fulfill racing requirements. Furthermore, the student documented process journal and time management deficits, noting irregular journaling, omitted reflection details, and compromised scheduling.

## Likely causes (source fact vs inference)
- Source facts: The student set a $250 budget without prior domain knowledge, quickly discovering racing-grade hardware, FPV gear, tools, and crash repairs required ~$1,500. The report admits neglecting the process journal and time management for MYP requirements.
- Inference: Scope optimism and failure to research total cost of ownership led to abandoning the financial constraint to salvage the functional product.

## Prevention lesson
Conduct upfront component and tooling cost audits for hardware projects, build financial buffers into criteria, and treat process documentation as a core weekly deliverable rather than an afterthought.
