# Phase 01: Define Project

## Objective

Define a focused, personally meaningful, and feasible Personal Project direction by establishing a working title, distinct learning and product goals, purpose, intended audience, and broad scope.

## Steps

1. Explore Interest Areas

> Identify and develop the personal interests, curiosities, experiences, and problems that could form the basis of the project.

2. Establish Learning Goal

> Define what I want to learn through the project and why that learning matters to me.

3. Establish Product Goal

> Define what I will create to demonstrate and apply the learning goal.

4. Gather Feedback

> Present the developing interest area, learning goal, and product goal to relevant feedback panels, then record useful feedback in `docs/feedback/`.

5. Define Project

> Revise the developing goals where appropriate, then establish the working title, purpose, intended audience, and broad scope in `docs/plans/project-definition.md`.
