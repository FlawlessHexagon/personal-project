# Phase 03: Research and Finalize Success Criteria

## Objective

Develop and finalize clear, measurable success criteria supported by relevant research.

## Steps

1. Identify Research Needs

> Review the draft success criteria and identify questions, gaps, and undecided elements requiring research.

2. Conduct Research

> Investigate relevant technical approaches and evaluation methods, documenting findings in the [process document](../../process/proc-success-criteria-development.md).

3. Outline Success Criteria

> Use the research findings to develop and justify the success criteria in the process document.

4. Finalize Success Criteria

> Refine the outlined criteria then consolidate them in the [final document](../../definition/success-criteria.md).
